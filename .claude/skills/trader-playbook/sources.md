## Press
- Financial Times — Markets, Alphaville, Unhedged (newsletter + podcast)
- Wall Street Journal — Markets, Heard on the Street
- Bloomberg — Markets; podcasts Odd Lots, Surveillance, Wall Street Week
- Reuters — Markets, Breakingviews
- The Economist — Finance & economics; podcast Money Talks
- CNBC — Fast Money, Halftime Report
- Europe: Il Sole 24 Ore, Milano Finanza, Handelsblatt, Les Echos
- Asia: Nikkei Asia, South China Morning Post (business)

## Trading / markets podcasts
- Chat With Traders
- Top Traders Unplugged
- Macro Voices
- Black Box (finance podcast; also FT Unhedged "Inside the traders' black box", Mar 2024)
