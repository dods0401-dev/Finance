## 1. The base rate: most day traders lose
- **Over 15 years of Taiwan data (1992–2006), day traders as a group lost money
  after costs every single year; under 1% were reliably profitable year after
  year.** — Barber, Lee, Liu, Odean, *Do Day Traders Rationally Learn About Their
  Ability?* (UC Berkeley working paper). **research**
  https://faculty.haas.berkeley.edu/odean/papers/day%20traders/day%20trading%20and%20learning%20110217.pdf
- **Brazilian futures day traders who kept at it over 300 days: about 97% lost
  money.** (Chague, De-Losso, Giovannetti, University of São Paulo) — widely
  quoted as "97% lose"; cited here second-hand. **research**
- **Traders with a history of losses keep trading almost as often as winners**
  (same Berkeley paper): the danger is not learning from your own record. → we
  keep a journal and review it weekly. **research**
- Implication for us: paper trade until the journal shows a positive average R
  over a meaningful sample (50+ trades), not a few good days.

## 2. Risk and position size
- **"Cut your losses, cut your losses, cut your losses"** — the rule repeated
  across Jack Schwager's *Market Wizards* interviews. **practitioner**
- **Size matters more than entry price; when unsure, halve the size you had in
  mind.** — attributed to Jack Schwager (Chat With Traders ep. 308, 29 Sep 2025,
  https://chatwithtraders.com/episode/ep-308-jack-schwager-emanuel-balarie). **practitioner**
- **Decide the exit before the entry** — removes emotion once in the trade (same
  episode). Our pack enforces this with `/trader-exit`. **practitioner**
- **Peter Brandt risks under 1% per trade** (Chat With Traders ep. 329, Aug 2026).
  Our 2% is at the upper end of the usual 1–2% range, hence the daily stop and
  the drawdown brake in `rules.md`. **practitioner**
- **Only take trades that pay at least ~2x the risk** — Ross Cameron's rule
  (podcast transcript). We use 1.5R minimum. **practitioner**
- A stop is a plan, not a guarantee: gaps and fast markets fill worse. **fact**

## 3. Behaviour
- Revenge trading after losses and overtrading are the classic account killers
  → hard stop after 2 losses in a row and max 3 trades/day. **practitioner**
- Write the reason before the trade; judge the process, not single outcomes. **practitioner**

## 4. Products to avoid while learning
- **Zero-day options: retail lost an estimated $358,000 a day** (University of
  Münster study, reported by Bloomberg, Apr 2023,
  https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2023-04-21/day-traders-lose-358-000-per-day-gambling-on-zero-day-options).
  Options are off in our rules. **research**
